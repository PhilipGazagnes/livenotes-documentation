# ADR-008: Decisions and notes live in `livenotes-documentation`

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Decision
- The second brain is this repo: plain Markdown, versioned with git, readable and writable by Claude.
- Decisions go in `app/decisions/` (this folder). Plans and roadmaps go in `app/strategy-2026.md` and specs.
- Obsidian is **not required**. It brings nothing for Claude. It can be used anytime as a viewer by opening this folder as a vault, with no changes needed.
